# Homepage planetary footage and photographs

The homepage uses actual spacecraft, space-station, and rover images. The clips are muted, cropped or resized for the layout, and compressed for web playback. The ISS aurora clip is a time lapse assembled from photographs. These images do not depict a Rldcoin transaction.

NASA content is used as factual visual context under the [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). NASA, JPL, and mission partners are not affiliated with and do not endorse Rldcoin. The depicted Earth, Jupiter, and Mars are all in our Solar System. Interstellar payments on the page are a future technical vision, not an operational route.
