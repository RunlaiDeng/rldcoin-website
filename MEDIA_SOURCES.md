# Homepage planetary footage and photographs

All homepage video and photographic backgrounds are excerpts or frames from actual spacecraft, space-station, or rover recordings. Clips are muted, cropped or resized for the layout, and compressed for web playback. The still posters and social preview are frames from those same sources. They do not depict a Rldcoin transaction.
