# Homepage launch footage

- Source: [Starlink 6-100 Launches from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Starlink_6-100_Launches_from_Cape_Canaveral_Space_Force_Station_(993274).webm>).
- Creator: U.S. Space Force video by Gwendolyn Kurzen. Wikimedia Commons identifies it as a U.S. federal government work in the public domain in the United States.
- Website use: a 16-second excerpt from the continuous launch shot, beginning two seconds into the source, resized to 1280 × 720, muted, with brief fades at the start and end. The poster and social preview use a frame from that excerpt.
- The footage provides visual context only. Rldcoin is independent of the launch provider and the U.S. Space Force.
