## Discriminating review and decision

The active hypothesis is that range-stable grants, per-object terminal control
and recursive cover statements remove the three preceding mechanism failures
without weakening currency conservation or permitting timeout refunds. The
review budget is one complete candidate plus at most two corrections per named
counterexample; no repeated protocol test campaign or new fixtures. A remaining
counterexample blocks publication of the affected claim. An engineering premise
is stated as a premise, never converted into a measured result.

### Disjoint range work

Counterexample in predecessor: one-slot claim [900,901) closes parent g and
invalidates pending work for [100,120), although the ranges are disjoint.
Revised rule: work binds G and selected bounds, not mutable segment identity or
bounds. Native split preserves G in both residuals. With cursor 10, after both
claims the resident ranges are [10,100), [120,900), [901,1000), whichever claim
is ordered first. Claim work inputs for the other range do not change. New Mint
frontiers may differ as ordered operations, but existing frontier anchors and
claim eligibility are preserved. Actual overlap, consuming a selected slot and
moving its authority legitimately invalidate that range. Returns create a new
grant only for returned slots; no detached identity is restored by timeout.

Decision: select stable grants; retire whole-parent-generation invalidation.
Observation is a direct source transition argument, not an executed race result.

### Proof production and ordinary-head progress

Counterexample in predecessor: candidate H must be proved before source debit;
ordinary extension changes its predecessor. A slow prover must retry or freeze
the global native head.
Revised rule: native Prepare first commits immutable I_x and delegates exact
escrow. A later proof targets that historical H_p. Native extension does not
change it. Controller terminal proof depends only on I_x and source creation,
with its own PBFT namespace, not the latest native head. Different controllers
have independent locks/positions. Before Exported, Canceled is directly executed
and certified without waiting for a recursive proof. The original native source
re-executes the bounded cancellation relation from known I_x and records its
single redemption. No ordinary native command can withdraw delegated escrow.

Cancel versus release: both candidate relations may be valid, but only a matching
terminal QC grants rights. Original-controller quorum intersection and durable
PBFT locks exclude both QCs under the fault assumption. A native handoff does
not revoke or replace that controller. After Exported, only permanent destination
rejection plus the source recovery map can return value. Proof/header/QC and
creation-ID order avoids self-referential commitments. I_x excludes its enclosing source checkpoint/operation digest and all later proof/QC wrappers. Terminal journals bind the source checkpoint externally. Checkpoint IDs commit complete resulting state/material roots; operation and stable identity preimages exclude only their specifically derived self-fields. Work preimages carry explicit protocol/currency tags.

Decision: select historical creation plus one-decision controllers. This removes
a mandatory global-head proving barrier; it does not remove shared CPU pressure,
finite admitted queues, controller quorum failures or proving cost.

### Long risk vectors and actual data

Counterexample in predecessor: a single component with 101 required labels cannot
meet a 100-label receiver limit even if its amount is split into many payments.
Revised representation: Covered(A) is a recursively verified statement that all
actual ancestry fits one immutable finite family policy. Native and controller
steps check new membership; ordinary epoch rotation under that family does not
append a live label. Same-A continuation needs current outputs, definitions and
access data plus the predecessor proof, not all detailed historical ancestry.

A-to-B broadening needs every input owner's explicit approval and verified rule
inclusion. Different covers are not silently merged, and unrelated change keeps
its own cover. A narrower/noncomparable cover cannot be derived from a broad
coverage certificate alone; it requires detailed witnesses or a willing exchange
of existing balances. Family incidents can quarantine all covered value. Thus
fixed current attribution trades precision for explicitly accepted conservative
exposure, rather than claiming lossless constant-size arbitrary history sets.

Decision: select finite explicit covers; preserve the interoperability/incident
tradeoff visibly in body, FAQ and explanatory pages. No universal acceptance or
risk deletion follows from this choice.

## Other reviewed obligations

- Recursive construction: distinct native/controller relations, historical source
  binding, exact journals, current matching QCs and resolved assumptions. Program
  image IDs must come from actual implementations before monetary use; none are
  fabricated in the paper or receipt. Correctness/soundness remain premises.
- Capacity: output/cover/object/byte/count limits, finite outstanding controllers,
  funded custody before preparation and separate fairly admitted service queues.
  Missing data is pending, not onward credit. Global permanent state still grows.
- Funding: finite reserves with named renewal payer and custody handoff. Deficits
  stop new obligations and retain pending old ones; insolvency is no refund proof.
- Ordering: PBFT and controller fault bounds are explicit. Public work and open
  carriage do not make arbitrary committees universally trusted.
- Issuance: exact slots/reward, proportional claim work, no administrator awards,
  no retarget/calendar promise, funded bounded work admission. Preparation fees
  use existing RLD; claims and Mint have no prior-balance prerequisite.
- Miner fees: positive owner-authorized existing-RLD work-author payment on every
  owner-initiated local/cross-domain payment, bound to a real prior Mint payout.
  It is not validator inflation or an unfunded prospective deduction.
- Keys: ordinary height seeds and independent controller pools have separate
  purposes. Validated public pools, one-use indices and original pending-object
  keys survive native handoff. Pool exhaustion stops new preparation, not old
  cancellation. Erasure, rollback protection and physical independence are
  assumptions requiring separate qualification.
- Encoding: deterministic typed CBOR, checked integers, exact signed allocation,
  ID preimages before attestations, currency-self-hash exception, state roots,
  inclusion/absence, namespace persistence and no partial mutation on rejection.
- Communication: real causal physical paths, forward receipt after setup, no
  compulsory live Earth directory, no immediate stellar finality. Data receipt,
  controller release, destination acceptance and onward spending remain distinct.

transitions, proof relations, conditional lemmas, finite resource/failure rules

specified conditional construction. It is not implementation completion or an
